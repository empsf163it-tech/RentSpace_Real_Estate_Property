Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate answers to common questions about our platform and listing verification.

How does RentSpace verify property listings?

Every listed property is reviewed against landmark coordinates, ownership credentials, and clear photo criteria before it receives an illustrative verification badge.

Is there any brokerage or registration fee?

No. Browsing, comparing properties, and contacting listing owners on RentSpace is completely free. We do not charge hidden booking or platform brokerage fees.

How can I compare multiple properties?

On any listing card in Search or Home, click the "Compare" button to add it to your shortlist. Then visit the Compare & List page to view all specs side by side.

How can property owners list their space?

Navigate to the Compare & List page and fill out the "List Your Property" form with photos, pricing, deposit, and contact details for prospective tenants.
